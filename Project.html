A Novel Hybrid Metaheuristic Framework for
Credit Risk Feature Selection in MSMEs
Integrating KS-statistic fitness with dynamic multi-swarm whale optimisation — reducing 88 MSME credit indicators to just 4, while preserving predictive AUC across six benchmark classifiers and validating across 30 independent runs (p < 0.001).
✦ Equal First Authors: Mohd Saad Choudhary & Naman Kumawat · Advisor: Dr. Mohit Sajwan

The AI PM Lens on this Research
Problem Framing
Identified the exact failure mode: fitness function mismatch. Raw error-rate doesn't work for imbalanced credit data. KS-statistic is threshold-independent — a decision that immediately de-risks deployment.
Stakeholder Impact
The 4-feature model means a credit analyst sees 4 inputs, not 88. Explainability goes from impossible to trivial. Regulatory compliance becomes feasible. This is PM work disguised as research.
Shipping Discipline
Seven-component validation, ablation study, dual-threshold evaluation, and honest reporting of a null result (+TMGWO). No inflated claims. That's the discipline of someone who has to maintain a product post-launch.

Philosophy

How I think about
AI products.

01
Fitness functions are product decisions.
What you optimise is what you get. In EMSWOA-KS, switching from error-rate to KS-statistic wasn't a technical tweak — it was a product pivot. Every ML model has a hidden fitness function. The PM's job is to make it explicit and align it with real-world cost.
02
Reduction is the feature, not the limitation.
Going from 88 to 4 indicators is the product. Interpretability, speed, regulatory clearance, analyst trust — all downstream of simplicity. The best AI products are aggressive editors. More inputs is rarely better UX for humans or models.
03
Honesty is a product moat.
Our ablation study reported a null result honestly — the TMGWO mutation did nothing. That kind of disciplined reporting is rare, and it's what makes a system trustworthy in production. Overpromised AI products fail in deployment. Honest ones compound.
